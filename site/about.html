Who we are

About Village Health Access

A humanitarian initiative, in partnership with Verakou, bringing quality healthcare to the most vulnerable communities in West Africa.

Our story

Born from what we saw firsthand.

Donald Daglo founded Village Health Access after seeing patients in Togo turned away from clinics because they could not afford care or could not reach it.

Families were choosing between food and medicine. People walked for hours to reach clinics that were closed or out of stock. Across Togo, Ghana, Benin and beyond, the gap in access to care is wide.

Village Health Access closes that gap with telemedicine, a network of partner pharmacies and community health workers, reaching people where they already are.
