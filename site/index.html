How it works

From a message to medicine, without the long walk.

  1. Reach out by WhatsApp

    Patients connect through WhatsApp and digital health platforms, in their own language. No clinic visit or travel is needed to start.

  2. Get triaged and seen

    A multilingual triage protocol routes each case to a licensed clinician for diagnosis, treatment and follow-up.

  3. Receive medicine

    Prescriptions are filled through our network of licensed partner pharmacies, at no cost to the patient.

Our mission

Care that doesn't depend on income, location or language.

We provide free, high-quality telemedicine and essential medicines to the most underserved communities in West Africa. We launched in Togo in 2026 and are building toward rural, refugee and urban-poor communities across the region.
